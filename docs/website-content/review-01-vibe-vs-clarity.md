# Review 01: Vibe vs. Clarity

**Date**: 2025-01-XX
**Source**: External Review
**Summary**: Site has killer vibe but makes people work too hard to understand what you do and why they should pay.

---

## What's Working

1. **Hero headline is strong**: "Your Data Is Cold. We Bring the Matches." is memorable and on-brand.
2. **CTA language is excellent**: "Initialize System Audit" feels like a ritual, not a consult form.
3. **Cohesive page**: Consistent typography, glow, grid, "protocol" framing.

---

## What's Costing Conversions (Fix First)

### 1. Legibility + Contrast
- A lot of body copy is tiny + low contrast on a textured background
- On anything less than a perfect monitor, it's mud
- The blur/glow effects are cool but stealing clarity from words that pay the bills

### 2. Too Much Apocalypse, Not Enough Outcomes
- "The end is near" is fun, but without specific wins, it reads like edgy branding cosplay
- Mostly identity ("we're the lab") and not enough receipt ("we shipped X, got Y result")

### 3. "Arsenal" Needs Proof, Fast
Need to show 3-6 capability cards with concrete outputs (not just vibes):
- "RAG Search that doesn't hallucinate" → "sources + eval harness + monitoring"
- "Agent workflows" → "guardrails + human-in-loop + audit logs"
- "Content reactor" → "pipeline + approvals + publishing schedule"

---

## Quick Wins (Ship in 1-2 Passes)

### Add 3-Bullet "We Build" Strip
Directly under hero paragraph (big, scannable, no fluff):
- AI systems that survive production (evals, monitoring, guardrails)
- Data pipelines that don't rot (cleaning, enrichment, retrieval)
- Automation that's accountable (audit logs, approvals, rollback)

### Add Trust Strip Under CTAs
- "Used by … / shipped for …" OR
- "10+ yrs shipping prod systems" OR
- "Case studies incoming" (but better: 1 real mini-case now)

### Make "System Audit" Tangible
Put a 3-line promise on the audit page/section:
- What they get (deliverables)
- How long it takes
- What it costs / what happens next

---

## Copy Tweaks (Keep Tone, Sharpen Blade)

**Current**: "AI Everywhere. Nowhere It Counts."
**Consider**: "AI is everywhere. Results aren't."

**Current**: "The end is near for the Specialist Class."
**Define in one sentence**: "If your business runs on 12 disconnected tools and tribal knowledge, you're already bleeding margin."

---

## UX / Implementation Notes

1. Tone down background FX behind paragraphs (or add subtle dark overlay panel behind text blocks)
2. Increase body font size + line-height; make paragraphs readable from 3 feet away
3. Ensure focus states and WCAG-ish contrast on buttons/links (audience includes 40+ buyers)

---

## Question for Clarification

**What's the primary goal?**
- Book audits?
- Sell productized "Ignition Protocol"?
- Build audience via Field Reports?

This will determine the above-the-fold conversion block rewrite.
