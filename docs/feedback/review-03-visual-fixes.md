## 🚨 CRITICAL ERROR: Invisible Daedalus Section

### The Issue
**Solution Section** (Introducing Daedalus) has dark grey text on black background. **Completely unreadable.**

### The Fix
Change text color of these elements to **White** or **Bright Orange**:
- "Introducing Daedalus"
- "Your Personal Military-Industrial Complex"

### Why This Matters
**This is the product reveal.** If they can't see it, they don't know what you're selling.

### Priority
**CRITICAL** - Fix immediately

## Design Philosophy Reminder

**Keep**: The strikethrough treatment, the tactical aesthetic, the military language
**Fix**: Readability issues that block conversion
**Add**: Specific promises (timelines, deliverables) without losing edge

The goal is not to soften the brand—it's to make the sharp edges **visible**.
